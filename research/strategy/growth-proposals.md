## Series names

Working labels for three editorial jobs. They are not separate brand identities.

| Job | Possible series | Reader benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Explain a consequential development | Kabar Mesin | Now I understand what changed. |
| Make a confusing concept understandable | Masuk Akal | Now I can explain this to someone else. |
| Examine an impressive claim | Cek Klaim | Now I know what to believe, and what remains uncertain. |

## First month

A starting experiment, not a required frequency. If four feed posts a week would weaken sourcing or design, the note says to start with two carousels and one Reel instead.

| Stage | Actions |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | Check Account Status; clarify the bio only if the owner adopts one; prepare pin choices; record a baseline once Insights exist; keep the five-hashtag rule |
| Week 2 | Three carousels: a consequential development, an explainer, and a claim check; test one faceless Reel |
| Week 3 | Repeat the strongest reader need; change one packaging variable at a time; collect questions in Stories |
| Week 4 | Compare similar posts; keep formats that can be sustained; plan the next month from those findings |

Stories, Trial Reels, carousel audio, and a 20–45 second Reel length are discussed in the research note. They are experiments in that note, not production requirements.
