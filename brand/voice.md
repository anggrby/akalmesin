All writing rules in one file. The machine-checked numbers live in [editorial-policy.json](editorial-policy.json) (checked by `npm run design:check`).

## Owner quick rules (index; the verbatim sections below are authoritative)

- Hook in the first 1–2 lines of the caption.
- Caption: exactly 3 body paragraphs, about 100–150 words, one closing question.
- Footer: `Sumber: X / Foto: Y`. Max 5 hashtags.
- kamu/kita allowed when natural.
- Bold one key phrase per detail slide; two on a benchmark slide.
- Write "benchmark", never "tolok ukur".
- AI illustration disclosure line: `Ilustrasi slide N: AI / akalmesin`.
- Relaxed, natural voice — not stiff/kaku (owner-confirmed 2026-10-28).
- Before drafting: read the [Terra example](examples/terra/README.md) and [style-feedback-log.md](style-feedback-log.md).

## akalmesin identity and positioning

Updated **27 October 2026**, Asia/Jakarta. This document owns the account's identity, audience and positioning. See [the repository map](../docs/workflow.md) for the authoritative source of each production rule.

### Identity and purpose

Write **akalmesin** as one lowercase word; Instagram is **@akalmesin**. The account serves Indonesian-speaking people in Indonesia who are curious about technology and are not assumed to be specialists. Coverage includes AI, robotics, computing, chips, software and related technology news.

akalmesin helps readers understand what changed, why it matters, and what the evidence supports. It is faceless editorial news media: the creator stays off camera, while relevant people and places may appear in reporting.

### Personality

Smart and approachable: clear, natural journalistic Indonesian with the judgment of an accessible editor or knowledgeable friend. Explain technical ideas in familiar terms and use a little wit when it fits. Retain the evidence, attribution and material limits.

[Editorial style](voice.md) is the authoritative writing guide for headlines, summaries, detail copy, captions, terminology, emphasis, depth and quotations. Read [the feedback log](style-feedback-log.md) before drafting to understand actual owner edits. Its historical comparisons do not override the current editorial guide.

The visual character is minimalist, photographic and editorial. [DESIGN.md](design.md) owns the complete visual specification. ## Indonesian news writing

Confirmed 25 October 2026. Updated **27 October 2026** for voice, captions, emphasis, wording, and the owner's growth research. Applies to headlines, summaries, body copy, captions, and quotation slides.

This is the authoritative writing guide for slide copy and captions, applying the owner's 27 October decisions. Machine-checked editorial limits are in [editorial-policy.json](editorial-policy.json); update the guide and policy together when the owner changes a rule. [The brand guide](voice.md) owns identity and positioning. Consult [style-feedback-log.md](style-feedback-log.md) for the original owner edit history when needed. Visual rules and measurement status belong in [DESIGN.md](design.md) and [typography evidence](design.md).

The 25 October update still supersedes the earlier casual educational-carousel voice. It does not ban the caption hook, the one closing question, or natural **kamu** / **kita** below.

### Voice

Write clear, natural journalistic Indonesian, like a knowledgeable friend or an accessible news editor who has read the primary source. The reader is curious about technology and is not assumed to be a specialist.

- Use **kamu** for direct address and **kita** for a shared implication when that is the natural phrasing. Do not force either.
- Explain a technical term when the sentence needs it. Keep a technical English term when it is the clearest name, and give the everyday context next to it.
- An occasional relatable observation is enough wit. Do not joke at the reader's expense, and do not add a joke to every post.
- Attribute claims. Distinguish an announcement, a test, a target, and a deployment. Keep the material caveat next to the claim it qualifies.
- Do not invent quotes, translate a competitor headline word for word, or add a save/share prompt.
- **Relaxed, not stiff** (owner-confirmed, 28 October 2026): he rejected compact, formal wording as "too kaku and unnatural". Write full conversational sentences; everyday loanwords (software, website, coding, context window) are fine; keep hedges in plain words. Examples: [style feedback log](style-feedback-log.md#kimi-k31-pro-2026-10-28-kimi-k3-1-pro-28-oktober-2026).

### Reader consequence

Before the copy is drafted, answer both:

- **What changes for the reader?**
- **Who would send this to whom, and why?**

A send might be a colleague weighing a tool, a friend who trusts a misleading demo, a student stuck on a term, or a team deciding whether a product changes their work. Those are angles. They are not invented quotes, and they are not a demand to go viral.

### Original contribution

A company announcement is source material, not the finished post. The akalmesin explanation has to be visible **inside the slides**, not only in the caption. A border, a watermark, subtitles, or a credit line do not by themselves make a repost original.

Use this test: **if the akalmesin contribution disappeared, would readers still learn almost the same thing?** If yes, add explanation, reporting, or perspective.

| Weak | Stronger contribution |
|---|---|
| A screenshot of a company's pricing chart | An explanation of what the price change means for different users |
| A reposted robot demonstration | What was autonomous, remote-operated, or only a controlled test, and what evidence is missing |
| A translated launch announcement | Indonesian availability, practical use, cost, and limits |
| A vendor benchmark with a new headline | What the benchmark does and does not prove |

Editing a source does not by itself give permission to use it. Keep provenance and any reuse limits in the story package.

### Structure

- **Headline:** name the subject and the concrete development in language a non-specialist can read. Prefer the specific factual headline the story needs; a question is optional. About 4–6 words is a starting point, not a target to force. The owner retained the longer factual headline in the [Terra example](examples/terra/README.md). Attribute claims that are not independently established. Avoid hype and vague suspense; do not shrink the type to fit a word count.
- **Slides:** one clear job each. Use as many as the explanation needs. That does not replace the depth rules below, and it is not a required five-slide sequence. When a longer carousel helps, a usable order is the development and its consequence, the main point, the mechanism or evidence, who is affected, then the limits and sources.
- **Summary on Main:** the most useful additional context or qualification, with about **6–9 words** as a flexible starting point. It should add to the headline, not repeat it. The owner-approved Terra summary is longer because its capabilities and price context matter. Edit copy before changing the owner's type scale.
- **Detail:** one reporting point, written as a news paragraph, figure, or explanation. See Emphasis below.
- **Quotation:** exact sourced words with speaker and role. Use only when the statement adds substance.

Owner's earlier headline example: **“Riset soroti peran Claude dalam keamanan kode”**. It is a tone reference for a factual headline, not a substitute for checking the story.

### Captions

Instagram shows about the first one or two lines before **selengkapnya**. Open there, in plain words, so a tech-curious reader is not met with a stiff official name.

Use **three paragraphs of about 100–150 words**. Count the paragraphs only, not the footer, the illustration line, or the hashtags.

1. **Hook and what is new.** A short observation or analogy grounded in the source, then the news: what happened, when, who, and the key numbers, with attribution. The hook names the question the post will answer. Do not open with vague suspense.
2. **Why it matters and how it works.** Plain language: what it is, what it costs or who can use it, and the concrete mechanism.
3. **The limit, the implication, and one question.** Scope, caveat, or stage of deployment, then **one** natural question. Do not add a second question or a save/share line.

**Footer:** `Sumber: X / Foto: Y`. Full source links stay in the story package and the Notion page body.

**Hashtags:** at most **five**. Include `#akalmesin` when it is useful for the archive, and let the other tags name this post. Do not spend longer on tags than on the explanation. Instagram's limit since December 2025 is in [the growth note](../research/strategy/2026-10-27-instagram-growth.md). An older copy of the owner's guide says 8–10. Do not use that figure.

**AI illustrations and synthetic media.** When any slide image is generated, add a line after the footer, in this form: `Ilustrasi slide 5: AI / akalmesin`. Name the slide that uses the illustration. The on-slide credit stays `ILUSTRASI AI: SUBJECT / AKALMESIN`. Never present a generated scene as documentary evidence. Check an AI-assisted treatment of a real photograph against the original. Realistic synthetic video or audio, including realistic generated narration, also needs the platform's required label. A caption credit does not replace that label.

### Emphasis

On each detail slide, bold the single most important phrase: the takeaway (who gets it, how much better, what it is for), not a model name and not the whole sentence.

- A benchmark slide may bold two phrases, the gain and the cost.
- A price slide gets one phrase, the comparison that carries the claim.

Mark the phrase in bold in `copy.md` and in the design-stage paragraph as `**important phrase**`. The HTML renderer displays that emphasis. In the live editor use Ctrl/⌘+B or type the markers; preserve the owner’s wording and check the final render.

### Wording

Use the loanword **benchmark**, not **tolok ukur**, in slide copy, captions, and alt text.

Differentiate `mengumumkan`, `menguji`, `menargetkan`, and `meluncurkan` according to the source. Attribute company statements with `menurut` or an equivalent. Distinguish shipment from sales, and global figures from Indonesian figures. A benchmark result is not general performance.

### Choosing depth

Use **Main + caption** for a self-contained brief. Expanded reports may add selected details. **In-depth coverage requires one Main plus 3–5 detail slides**, each with a distinct source-supported reporting point. A verified quote is optional and additional; it does not count as one of the details. If the evidence supports only one or two useful details, keep the report expanded rather than padding it or labeling it in-depth.

### Quote provenance

For every quote, save the original wording, speaker, role at the time, original source URL, date, and timestamp/page if relevant. Check context and whether an omission changes the meaning. An Indonesian translation must be faithful and identified in the caption/source record as a translation. A paraphrase is body copy and must not appear as an exact quote. Never treat sample text from a layout or an AI-generated sentence as a person's words.

### Owner-reviewed example

The [Terra example](examples/terra/README.md) contains the actual attached PNGs, slide text, alt text, source record, and exact Notion Caption property. The earlier body caption differs; the example records that discrepancy. Use this as calibration, alongside explicit owner choices and the source of each new story. One post does not establish an absolute rule for every headline or detail.

### Caption handoff

Each package should identify its story format, source-supported claims, relevant dates, caption, per-image alt text, photo credit, and unresolved reporting questions. Retain full source links in the package. Notion delivery does not publish the post. Scheduling is the later step in [docs/workflow.md](../docs/workflow.md), and it still needs the owner's date and time.
