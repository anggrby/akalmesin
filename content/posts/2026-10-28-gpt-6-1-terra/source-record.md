## Verified facts

- Launch & availability: Plus, Pro, Enterprise, Edu in ChatGPT Work and Codex; API `gpt-6.1-terra` \[A\]\[B\]\[X4\].
- Standard prices per 1M tokens (input / cached / output), ≤272K input context \[D\]: GPT-6.1 Terra \$1 / \$0.05 / \$5; GPT-6.1 Sol \$2 / \$0.10 / \$10; GPT-6 Astra \$10 / \$1 / \$50; GPT-6 Luna \$0.10 / \$0.01 / \$0.50; GPT-5.6 Terra \$2 / \$0.20 / \$12. \>272K context Terra: \$2 / \$0.10 / \$7.50. Batch/Flex half price; regional endpoints +10%.
- "50% lower": exactly 50% vs GPT-6.1 Sol on all three prices; vs GPT-5.6 Terra input and cached input -50%, output -58.3% (\$12 → \$5). Computed from \[D\].
- Usage allowance: goes further than GPT-5.6 Terra, "far more" (X2) / "substantially more" (A) than GPT-6.1 Sol or GPT-6 Astra.
- OpenAI recommends GPT-6.1 Sol or GPT-6 Astra for in-depth research or complex coding \[X2\]\[A\].
- OpenAI benchmarks (self-reported): Agents' Last Exam +15 pp vs GPT-5.6 Terra at \~1/3 cost, on par with GPT-6 Astra at 1/10 price per token, \>3x faster than Astra and Sol \[A\]\[X3\]; Terminal-Bench 4.0 50.3% vs 21.5% \[A\]; internal error rate (low effort) 53.6% → 16.7% \[A\].
- Safety: meets "Critical" cybersecurity threshold in Preparedness Framework; keeps GPT-6 Astra protections \[A\]\[C\].
- Specs: 1,050,000 context, 128K max output, text+image in, text out, knowledge cutoff 1 Jun 2026 \[B\].
