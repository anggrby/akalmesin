## Judgement calls

- Loss figure: sources range US$6.3B (BBC, Verge) to US$6.34B (SecurityWeek; Hacker News says 6.43). Copy uses "sekitar US$6,3 miliar".
- Attribution stated as FBI's attribution; North Korea's denial included (slide 4 and caption).
- Recovery: Binance's own percentages conflict (98% as of 31 Oct vs 95% in a later update per Blockchain.News). Copy uses "98%" attributed to Binance ("mengklaim").
- The sources don't explain exactly how the AI tool was used to take over the laptops; copy only says it was used "untuk mengendalikan laptop dua karyawan lain" (BBC).
- Slide 3 photo is a generic illustration (credit ILUSTRASI); the main photo of Teng is from 2023, cropped to drop the outdated on-screen title.
