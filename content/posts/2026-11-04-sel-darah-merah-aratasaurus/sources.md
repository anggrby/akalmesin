## Judgement calls

- Framed as a claim throughout ("mengklaim"); skeptics slide 04 and caption name the three objections. The team's own preservation explanation (hausmannite, rapid burial) is left out to keep the carousel short; not contradicted.
- "Komponen hemoglobin" is a plain-language gloss for heme.
- Main image is an artist's reconstruction (credit FOTO: ILUSTRASI). Slide 04 shows Schweitzer's mobile lab (2009), not the skeptics themselves: no free photo of Schweitzer, Saitta or McNamara on Commons. Weakest photo of the set.
