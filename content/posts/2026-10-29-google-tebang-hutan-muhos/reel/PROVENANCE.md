# Reel provenance — Google tebang hutan Muhos (retrieved 2026-10-29 WIB)

Media is used **as-is**: scaled and cropped to 9:16 with a slow pan on stills, trimmed and resampled to 30 fps. There is no colour filter and no blue ink. scripts/reel/check.mjs verifies the recorded ffmpeg graphs (gate "no-filter"). The overlays (headline card, tags, map, bars, counter, end card) are original HTML/SVG in brand colours. The Finland outline comes from Natural Earth (public domain) via scripts/reel/data/finland.geo.json. **No licensed footage of the actual Muhos site exists.** The drone and harvester clips are illustrative and labelled "(ilustrasi)" on screen.

## Brand mark (v3)

No logo image in the reel. The brand appears as the plain text "akalmesin" (Manrope 800, 46 px) at the bottom of the frame, white or black per beat (pinned in reel.json, chosen by worst-case WCAG contrast against the background), and as the animated text in the 2 s outro (black background).

## Voice

v2 (current): **no voice.** The captions narrate.

v1 (superseded, kept as /workspace/finland-forest-reel-voice-v1.mp4): edge-tts 7.3.1 (MIT client) with Microsoft id-ID-GadisNeural through the Edge Read Aloud service. Microsoft publishes no commercial-use licence for that service, so check this before a paid or branded post, or switch to Azure AI Speech (same voice, licensed). Piper id_ID-news_tts was checked: its model card gives the licence only as "See URL", which points to an unrelated Kaggle corpus, and the model is fine-tuned from lessac. The terms are no clearer, so it was not used.
