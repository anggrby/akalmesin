# Reel provenance — Google tebang hutan Muhos (retrieved 2026-10-29 WIB)

Media is used **as-is**: scaled and cropped to 9:16 with a slow pan on stills, trimmed and resampled to 30 fps. There is no colour filter and no blue ink. scripts/reel/check.mjs verifies the recorded ffmpeg graphs (gate "no-filter"). The overlays (headline card, tags, map, bars, counter, end card) are original HTML/SVG in brand colours. The Finland outline comes from Natural Earth (public domain) via scripts/reel/data/finland.geo.json. **No licensed footage of the actual Muhos site exists.** The drone and harvester clips are illustrative and labelled "(ilustrasi)" on screen.
