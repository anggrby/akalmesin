# Provenance — Google tebang hutan Muhos (retrieved 2026-10-29 WIB)

All five are real photographs from Wikimedia Commons. Originals are unmodified in originals/, and each download's SHA-1 matches the one Commons lists. They were processed with scripts/blue-ink.mjs (crop and maxWidth set in post.json, no generative edits). The Main photo is not reused on any other slide. **None of the clear-cut photos show the Muhos site.** They are generic photos of clear-cuts in Finland. Only the town hall (slide 2) and Pyhäkoski (slide 5) photos were taken in Muhos.
