# Sources — Antrean listrik data center Finlandia (retrieved 2026-10-29 11:40 WIB)

The story: on 23 Oct 2026 Fingrid (Finland's transmission system operator) said grid-connection applications, mostly from data centres and storage, are flooding in before a planned priority-procedure law, so not all can be processed this year. Picked because it's the most recent primary-source "Finland data centers" development (6 days old), with a clear mechanism. Other recent items: Google ordered to halt Muhos/Kajaani site work (LVV, 6–8 Oct); local appeals in Lapinlahti (23 Oct); Helsinki considering 2–3 sites (18 Oct).

Primary: Fingrid press release 23.10.2026 (Finnish), https://www.fingrid.fi/ajankohtaista/tiedotteet/2026/liittymissopimushakemusten-kasittely-ruuhkautunut/ (FG). TEM/Valtioneuvosto consultation release 18.9.2026, https://valtioneuvosto.fi/-/1410877/sahkoverkkoon-liittamisen-etusijamenettely-lausuntokierrokselle (TEM). Secondary: HPP law-firm summary of the draft (25 Sep), Borenius legal alert (27 Oct) and Valpas, both citing TEM's 23 Oct release; Ilta-Sanomat (23 Oct); Yle News (7 Oct).

Status meanings: "Verified" = stated in the primary source. "Draft" = in the draft bill, which can still change. "Secondary" = only from a reliable secondary source; the primary wasn't opened.
